Troubleshooting and safety

Safety first

  • The Workbench and its firmware are experimental: not certified for production or safety-critical use.
  • Keep a physical emergency stop in the motor power circuit, and wire an estop input (A-9 unless you pick another) (Board page, Estop).
  • Run every new profile in Simulate first, then with Hand jog, then slowly.
  • Keep motor speeds well inside the motors' ratings (Analysis shows them).
